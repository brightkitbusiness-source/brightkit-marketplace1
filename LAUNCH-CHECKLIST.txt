BRIGHTKIT MARKETPLACE — LAUNCH CHECKLIST

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Responsive marketplace homepage and product catalogue
- Five collections: Social & Creators; Business & Finance; Career & Education; Restaurants & Hospitality; Lifestyle & Travel
- 15 sample product listings: 10 individual products and 5 bundles
- Search, category filters, individual/bundle tabs, price sorting and a working demo cart
- Language selector: English, Macedonian, French, German, Russian, Spanish, Mexican Spanish, Italian and Portuguese
- EUR sample pricing

IMPORTANT: THIS IS A FRONT-END STORE DEMO
The listed products are product concepts and sample descriptions. The actual downloadable files still need to be created, quality-checked and uploaded. Payment processing, automatic delivery, customer accounts, order management and legal pages are not connected. The cart is a local demo only; checkout intentionally does not take payments.

HOW TO LAUNCH WITH A ZERO HOSTING BUDGET
1. Create a free GitHub account and a repository named brightkit.
2. Upload index.html to the repository root.
3. In Settings → Pages, deploy from the main branch and root folder.
4. Use the public GitHub Pages URL provided after deployment.

PAYMENTS AND AUTOMATIC DOWNLOADS
1. Compare digital-download platforms such as Payhip or Gumroad and verify current seller eligibility, payout support for North Macedonia, fees, tax handling and terms before choosing.
2. Create each product listing on the selected platform and upload the real files.
3. Copy each product's checkout URL.
4. Replace each product card's demo Add to cart flow with its corresponding secure checkout link, or integrate a supported storefront checkout.
5. Test a complete purchase and download from another device before announcing the shop.

BEFORE ACCEPTING CUSTOMERS
- Create and upload every promised template; do not sell placeholders.
- Add business contact details, privacy policy, terms of sale, refund policy and product-specific licences.
- Check local business registration, tax, VAT/digital-sales and consumer-protection obligations with an appropriate professional.
- Proofread all translations. The current version has full English and Macedonian interface copy; other languages appear in the selector but fall back to English until professional translations are completed.
- Set realistic product licences and do not claim rights you cannot grant.
- Test mobile layout, links, prices, checkout and file delivery.

GROWTH
Start with the 15 focused listings, publish useful demonstrations, collect honest feedback, then expand the best-performing categories. Traffic and sales are not guaranteed; a passive-income store still needs products, marketing and customer support.
